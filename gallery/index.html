    Light and dark

    Light is the default theme. Dark is its own palette with warm black and amber, not an inverted copy. Shown on the Backpack design mockups.

    Lootsmith is an unofficial fan project. It is licensed under Zlib and ships no Gearbox art. It is a Rust port based on Gibbed's Borderlands 2 Save Editor by Rick Gibbed (zlib).

    Fonts are Pixelify Sans and VT323, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Made with the assistance of AI and primarily for personal use. Use at your own risk.
